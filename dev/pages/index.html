cyrus

A faithful rebuild of The Elder Scrolls Adventures: Redguard (1998) in Rust and Bevy.

Bring your own copy of Redguard from GOG. cyrus reads your install and includes no game files.

Early development. Worlds, menus, and movies are available to explore; full gameplay is not yet supported.

What works today

  • The installed splash and title, then the data-driven book menu.
  • Unlocked movies, persistent settings, and New Game into the starting world.
  • Redguard worlds loaded from a local GOG install in the native or browser viewer, with a free camera.

Faithful by default

Faithful mode reproduces what the player experiences: visuals, audio, controls, camera, script and animation timing, and outcomes. Technical limits are not part of that goal; cyrus loads faster and runs at higher frame rates without changing game behaviour. Modern controls and graphics remain opt-in.

File formats and engine documentation come from redguard-preservation. RGUnity is another project rebuilding Redguard, using the Unity engine. It inspired cyrus, and its contributors' research helped us understand how the original game works.
